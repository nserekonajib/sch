How Division Works

Division 1 (4-12 points)

Must pass all four subjects with at least a Credit (C6) or better. A single F9 in any subject automatically drops the candidate out of Division 1.

Division 2 (13-23 points)

Must pass English and Mathematics with at least a Pass (P8) or better.

Division 3 (24-29 points)

Basic pass levels in core subjects. Even if aggregates are low, getting an F9 in multiple subjects pushes the student here.

Division 4 (30-34 points)

The absolute minimum pass level to receive a certificate. Must pass at least two subjects.

Ungraded (35-36 points)

Awarded if a candidate fails all subjects (F9) or does not meet the minimum requirements for Division 4. Does not qualify for secondary school.
